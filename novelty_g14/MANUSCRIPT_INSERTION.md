## Evidence availability and limits of inference (primary cohort)

Of the 2,352 outcomes with unknown eventual recovery in the fixed 853-session cohort, 936 have publicly released excerpt records. The total exact weight of these records is 41.60% of the unrestricted identification interval width. Perfect ascertainment confined to this subset cannot identify the sign of the recovery contrast. Among sessions without released excerpts, 97 additional sessions contain at least one source-validation rejection attributable to invisible execution or termination before completion. Their 271 unknown episodes form a disjoint candidate ascertainment frame. Under hypothetical perfect ascertainment of the two sets, 53.94% of the original width is removable. The rejection signal is not a repair-failure label, proof of access, or demonstrated audit efficiency.

## External execution-based challenge (separate cohort)

Using the six settings of Cim, Topcu and Kandemir's Termination Lag release, we linked conservative positive local test receipts to the submitted recorded patch identifier. Of 142 qualifying runs, 87 were nonpassing under the source benchmark; requiring three adjacent matching patch snapshots retained 70 nonpasses among 113 runs. These are source-provided verdicts, not new full-benchmark reruns. Run units can share benchmark tasks, and the narrow pytest/unittest screen restricts coverage.

In the Verified settings, a fixed subset of 13 distinct tasks contained a positive receipt reporting every required issue-repair test name. Static reconstruction at the original base commits found 14 of 15 named test-function definitions structurally different from the reference version. This difference was present in all seven source benchmark passes and five of six nonpasses, so it is not a failure classifier. We then independently executed a controlled five-cell test-file-version crossover on the frozen 13-case frame in isolated benchmark images. Ten candidate local/reference pairs had determinate outcomes and identical staged non-test-tree digests: nine passed both versions, and one passed locally but failed with the reference test files. The latter outcome was reproduced on the same image and patch, with the base failing and the developer reference solution passing the reference test. Three pairs remained unresolved. In seven cases, the local tests passed both base and candidate, while reference tests distinguished them (base failed, candidate and gold passed). These observations support a bounded existence claim: identical test names and positive local results do not certify transport of the claim to a different test definition. They do not establish a population failure rate, universal semantic inequivalence, or the cause of historical benchmark nonpass.

## Limitations and provenance (supplementary)

One historical nonpass passed all six named repair and preservation tests in the new harness; another passed 42 collected items but left 13 of 25 required preservation-test identifiers unresolved. The latter is not a complete evaluation. Two candidate patches could not be applied exactly, and a third candidate's reference test collection failed with an import error; its gold control was also unresolved. All three remain HOLD. In the whole archive, 26,448 of 26,666 patch rows agree with the SHA1-prefix convention used in the upstream grading code. Of 218 mismatches, 211 have declared dependency-file removals; seven are unexplained. All 13 selected candidate patches agree with the convention and have zero declared removals, yet one fails a non-applying syntax check and another fails exact contextual application. Among 149 same-task exact-released-byte duplicate groups, no mixed source grades were found. Digest consistency, exact applicability, test identity and outcome validity are distinct verification objects.

A distinct external official-submission archive challenge admitted 1,375 of 1,500 run units. Of 242 runs with a recognized historical local positive marker, 65 were benchmark nonpasses (56 issue-test-only, one preservation-test-only, eight both); the local receipt is not necessarily bound to the submitted state. Do not pool this archive with the observational or controlled crossover denominators.
