## Candidate main-text insertion: evidence availability and external verification

In the fixed 853-session observational cohort, 936 of the 2,352 unknown outcomes have public excerpt records. These account for 41.60% of the exact width of the unrestricted recovery-contrast identification interval. Even perfect ascertainment confined to those records cannot identify its sign. A separate, source-validation-stage signal (a rejected claim because execution was not visible or the session terminated before completion) identifies 97 additional matched sessions without public excerpts, containing 271 unknown outcomes. Under the explicitly hypothetical perfect ascertainment of both disjoint sets, 53.94% of the initial width is removable. This is a conditional capacity calculation, not a realized evidence acquisition result, a causal effect, or a proof of legal access.

An external execution-based challenge illustrates why apparently positive evidence cannot automatically be transported to an expanded claim. In six settings from Cim, Topcu and Kandemir's Termination Lag release (2,400 run units), 142 runs had a conservative local positive test marker linked to the submitted recorded patch ID; 87 did not pass the source benchmark. Three adjacent matching patch snapshots retained 70 nonpasses among 113 qualifying runs. These source grades are not independently rerun full benchmark outcomes; tasks can repeat across settings and local markers are screened for a narrow command/output format.

Within the Verified settings, 13 fixed runs contained one recorded positive receipt listing all required issue-repair test names. Static reconstruction found a difference from the reference named-function AST in 14 of 15 definitions. The difference is not a failure classifier: it occurred in all seven historical benchmark passes and five of six nonpasses. In an isolated five-cell crossover of this fixed cohort, ten candidate local/reference pairs were determinate under equal recorded non-test trees: nine passed both test-file versions, and one passed locally but failed under the reference test-file version. The latter was reproduced on the same image and patch; the base failed the reference test and the developer gold passed it. Three pairs remain unresolved. This provides a controlled existence witness of non-transportable positive test evidence, not a prevalence estimate or a complete historical failure explanation.
